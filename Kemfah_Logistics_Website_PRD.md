# **1\. Executive Summary**

Kemfah Logistics Services Limited is a Nigerian private company built with a clear mandate: to close the gaps in Nigeria's logistics and cargo movement infrastructure. The company operates across logistics and freight — including road haulage, marine transport, international air cargo, and clearing and forwarding — with additional lines in import/export trading and general supply.

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the complete scope for the design and development of Kemfah's corporate website. It covers business context, site architecture, page-by-page content requirements, copywriting guidance, image requirements, and functional specifications.

## **1.1 Business Context**

| Company Name:   Kemfah Logistics Services Limited Company Type:   Private Company Limited by Shares — CAC Licensed Market:         Nigeria (nationwide) with international cargo and trade corridors Primary Sector: Road Freight, Marine Transport, International Air Cargo, Clearing & Forwarding Global Reach:   IATA-aligned air cargo facilitation — international freight documentation and routing Theme:          Navy blue dominant — professional, global, trustworthy. Amber accent on CTAs only. |

# **5\. Brand & Visual Direction**

## **5.1 Colour Palette — Navy-Dominant Theme**

Following the client directive, Navy Blue is the dominant theme colour. Navy is used as the background surface for all major structural sections (navigation, hero, stats bar, CTA strips, footer). White and light blue become content surfaces that provide breathing space between navy blocks. Amber shifts to the primary action colour, used on CTA buttons to create strong contrast against navy.

| Colour | Hex | Hierarchy | Role | Used On |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Deep Navy** | **\#1B3A6B** | **DOMINANT** | Theme background | *Navbar bg, hero bg, stats bar bg, section bgs, page anchors* |
| **Midnight Navy** | **\#0F2347** | **DEPTH** | Dark surfaces | *Footer bg, hover states on nav links, dropdown menus* |
| **Amber Gold** | **\#E8891A** | **PRIMARY ACTION** | CTA & accent | *CTA buttons on navy, CTA strip background, icon accents on cards, divider lines* |
| **White** | **\#FFFFFF** | **CONTENT BASE** | Light surfaces | *Text on navy, service cards, about section, contact form, inputs* |
| **Sky Blue** | **\#EDF3FA** | **SECONDARY** | Soft separator | *Alternating content section bg, info box fills, form focus states* |
| **Charcoal** | **\#1A1A2E** | **BODY TEXT** | Dark text | *Body paragraphs, card subtitles, form labels on white sections* |

| Key rule:  Never place dark charcoal text directly on a navy background — always use white. Never use navy as a text colour on a navy section. Amber is the ONLY colour that should appear as a button or interactive element on navy surfaces. Section rhythm:  Navy section → White/light content section → Navy section → Amber CTA strip → White content → Footer (deep navy). This alternating rhythm creates visual depth and prevents navy fatigue. |
| :---- |

## **5.2 Typography**

| Primary Font:    Inter or Poppins (Google Fonts — free) On navy sections:Headings \= White \#FFFFFF, body text \= White at 80% opacity, subheadings \= White at 65% opacity On white sections:Headings \= Deep Navy \#1B3A6B, body text \= Charcoal \#1A1A2E, muted text \= mid-grey CTA Buttons:     Amber background \#E8891A, white text — used on all navy sections. Navy bg, white text — used on amber CTA strips. Font sizes:      H1: 48–56px · H2: 32–36px · H3: 22–24px · Body: 16–18px · Small/label: 13–14px Fallback:        Arial, sans-serif (for email renders and print) |
| :---- |

## **5.3 Brand Voice & Tone**

* Professional but approachable — not corporate-cold, not casual

* Action-oriented — use strong verbs: ship, deliver, connect, book, move

* Trust-forward — every page should reinforce legal registration and reliability

* Nigerian-authentic — address the local market directly; avoid generic Western copy

* Clear and direct — no jargon; a first-time visitor must understand the offer immediately

## **5.4 Logo Specifications & Usage Guide**

The Kemfah Logistics logo consists of two elements: (1) a globe icon with a sweeping orbital arrow passing through it from bottom-left to upper-right, and (2) wordmark text — 'KEMFAH' in large bold uppercase, 'LOGISTICS SERVICES' in smaller bold uppercase below. The arrow direction communicates forward movement and global reach — core to the brand's identity.

| Theme change impact:  With navy as the dominant theme, the Reversed (white) logo variant is now the DEFAULT — it appears on the navigation bar, hero section, and all navy-background sections. The dark (navy \+ amber) version is secondary, used only on white content sections. New variant required: A navy \+ amber logo on an amber/white background is needed for the CTA strips, where the background is amber (\#E8891A). |
| :---- |
